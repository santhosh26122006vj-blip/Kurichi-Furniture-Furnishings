About Kurichi

Furniture that makes a space feel like yours.

Kurichi Furniture & Furnishings is a furniture and furnishings store in Madurai, offering a range of pieces across living room, bedroom, dining, sofas, office and furnishing categories. The showroom is arranged so you can walk through complete settings, compare materials and finishes, and get a real sense of scale and comfort before you decide.

Every visit is guided at your pace — browse freely, ask questions, and take the time you need with pieces that will live in your home for years.
